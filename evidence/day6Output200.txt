ANSWER:
A common approach is to use 10 to 20 percent overlap between chunks. Overlap matters because it prevents information from being cut off at chunk boundaries. If a sentence spans two chunks, overlap ensures the full meaning appears in at least one chunk. Without overlap, retrieval quality drops on queries that depend on cross-boundary context.
