  [score=0.4142] approach is to use chunk sizes of 256 to 512 tokens with 10 to 20 percent overla...
  [score=0.2159] Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that combines information re...

ANSWER:
Chunk overlap is commonly set at 10 to 20 percent between chunks. It matters because it prevents information from being cut off at chunk boundaries, ensuring that if a sentence spans two chunks, its full meaning appears in at least one chunk. Without overlap, retrieval quality drops on queries that depend on cross-boundary context.
